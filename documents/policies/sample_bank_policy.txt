Sample Internal Banking Policy

1. Money Transfers

Customers may initiate transfers between eligible accounts.

2. Transfer Approval

Transactions exceeding configured limits may require additional verification or approval.

3. Failed Transactions

Failed transactions must provide an appropriate failure status to the customer.

4. Transaction Records

Completed and failed transactions should be recorded for operational and audit purposes.

5. Customer Support

Customers should be able to obtain information about the status of their transactions.

IMPORTANT:
This document is a fictional internal policy created for the academic project.
