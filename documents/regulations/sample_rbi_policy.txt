Sample Financial Regulation Reference

Document: Digital Banking Transaction Controls
Source: Sample regulatory reference for project demonstration

1. Customer Authentication

Financial transactions shall require customer authentication before execution.

2. Transaction Authorization

The system shall verify that the authenticated customer is authorized to perform the requested transaction.

3. Transaction Limits

Financial institutions may define transaction limits based on account type, transaction type, customer profile, and risk level.

4. Audit Logging

Financial transaction activities should be recorded in an audit trail containing sufficient information to support investigation and compliance review.

5. Data Protection

Sensitive customer and transaction information shall be protected against unauthorized access and disclosure.

6. Fraud Monitoring

Systems handling financial transactions should support monitoring mechanisms for detecting potentially suspicious or fraudulent activity.

7. Record Retention

Relevant transaction and audit records should be retained according to applicable organizational and regulatory retention requirements.
